# Balance report

Generated by `npm run sim` (scripts/sim.ts): 840 games, 100 quarters each, 3 seeds per cell,
industries: saas, retail, mfg, logistics, energy, consulting, biotech. Took 23 s.

Each game is one scripted player against the rival AIs. Bots use only the actions a human has.
- **Passive**: Does nothing at all. The floor.
- **Steady**: Profitable growth, no outside money until it is cheap.
- **Blitz**: Venture-funded land grab: raise, hire, expand, worry later.
- **Premium**: Out-invest on R&D and charge for it.
- **Leveraged**: Debt-funded expansion; keeps all the equity.
- **Gouge**: Probe: maximum price, minimum staff, harvest cash.
- **Flipper**: Probe: raise every round offered and sit on the cash.
- **Warmonger**: Probe: sue and poach every rival, every chance it gets.
- **Raider**: Probe: borrow and raise to buy rivals outright.
- **Empire**: Probe: open in every city as fast as cash allows.
